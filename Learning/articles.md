## AI

- **[A Recipe for Training Neural Networks](https://karpathy.github.io/2019/04/25/recipe/)** – Andrej Karpathy; practical step-by-step advice for training models that work.
- **[Machines of Loving Grace](https://www.darioamodei.com/essay/machines-of-loving-grace)** – Dario Amodei; an optimistic look at how AI could transform the world.
- **[Software 2.0](https://karpathy.medium.com/software-2-0-a64152b37c35)** – Andrej Karpathy; why neural networks are a new way of writing software.
- **[The Bitter Lesson](http://incompleteideas.net/IncIdeas/BitterLesson.html)** – Richard Sutton; why general methods plus compute beat hand-built knowledge.
- **[The Illustrated Transformer](https://jalammar.github.io/illustrated-transformer/)** – Jay Alammar; the clearest visual explanation of how Transformers work.
- **[Understanding LSTM Networks](https://colah.github.io/posts/2015-08-Understanding-LSTMs/)** – Chris Olah; a famous, intuitive walkthrough of LSTMs.

## Career & Craft

- **[How to Do Great Work](https://paulgraham.com/greatwork.html)** – Paul Graham; on choosing what to work on and doing it exceptionally.
- **[Teach Yourself Programming in Ten Years](https://norvig.com/21-days.html)** – Peter Norvig; why real skill takes years, not days.
- **[You and Your Research](https://www.cs.virginia.edu/~robins/YouAndYourResearch.html)** – Richard Hamming; a classic talk on how to do important work.

## Engineering

- **[Choose Boring Technology](https://mcfunley.com/choose-boring-technology)** – Dan McKinley; why proven tools usually beat shiny new ones.
- **[Falsehoods Programmers Believe About Names](https://www.kalzumeus.com/2010/06/17/falsehoods-programmers-believe-about-names/)** – Patrick McKenzie; the many wrong assumptions we make about real-world data.
- **[Latency Numbers Every Programmer Should Know](https://colin-scott.github.io/personal_website/research/interactive_latency.html)** – Interactive chart of how long common computer operations take.
- **[What Every Programmer Should Know About Memory](https://www.akkadia.org/drepper/cpumemory.pdf)** – Ulrich Drepper; deep dive into how memory and CPU caches really work.

## Startups & Ideas

- **[1,000 True Fans](https://kk.org/thetechnium/1000-true-fans/)** – Kevin Kelly; why a creator only needs a thousand true fans to make a living.

## AI & Machine Learning

- **[Anthropic Courses](https://anthropic.skilljar.com)** – Free self-paced courses from Anthropic on using and building with Claude.
- **[CS224N: NLP with Deep Learning](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs224n/)** – Stanford's course on language models and NLP.
- **[CS231n: Deep Learning for Computer Vision](https://cs231n.stanford.edu)** – Stanford's classic deep learning course, with free notes.
- **[DeepLearning.AI](https://www.deeplearning.ai)** – Andrew Ng's courses and short courses on AI, LLMs and agents.
- **[Elements of AI](https://www.elementsofai.com)** – Free, non-technical introduction to AI from the University of Helsinki.
- **[Google Machine Learning Crash Course](https://developers.google.com/machine-learning/crash-course)** – Google's free, fast-paced intro to machine learning.
- **[Hugging Face Learn](https://huggingface.co/learn)** – Free courses on LLMs, agents, diffusion and more.
- **[Kaggle Learn](https://www.kaggle.com/learn)** – Short, free, hands-on lessons in Python, pandas and machine learning.
- **[Neural Networks: Zero to Hero](https://karpathy.ai/zero-to-hero.html)** – Andrej Karpathy builds neural networks and GPT from scratch, step by step.
- **[Practical Deep Learning for Coders](https://course.fast.ai)** – fast.ai's free, top-down course for programmers.

## Computer Science

- **[CS50](https://cs50.harvard.edu)** – Harvard's famous intro to computer science, free online.
- **[MIT OpenCourseWare](https://ocw.mit.edu)** – Free lecture notes, videos and assignments from thousands of MIT courses.
- **[Nand2Tetris](https://www.nand2tetris.org)** – Build a whole computer, from logic gates to an operating system.
- **[NPTEL](https://nptel.ac.in)** – Free courses from the IITs and IISc across engineering and science.
- **[Teach Yourself Computer Science](https://teachyourselfcs.com)** – A self-study path through the core CS subjects with the best books and lectures.
- **[The Missing Semester of Your CS Education](https://missing.csail.mit.edu)** – MIT course on practical tools like the shell, Git and editors.

## Math

- **[3Blue1Brown](https://www.3blue1brown.com)** – Beautiful visual explanations of math, including linear algebra and neural networks.
- **[Khan Academy](https://www.khanacademy.org)** – Free lessons in math, science and more, from basics to college level.

## Programming & Web Development

- **[Exercism](https://exercism.org)** – Free coding exercises in 70+ languages, with mentoring.
- **[freeCodeCamp](https://www.freecodecamp.org)** – Free interactive curriculum for web development and more.
- **[roadmap.sh](https://roadmap.sh)** – Step-by-step roadmaps for frontend, backend, AI and other developer roles.
- **[The Odin Project](https://www.theodinproject.com)** – Free, project-based full-stack web development curriculum.

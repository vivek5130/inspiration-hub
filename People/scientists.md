- **[Andrej Karpathy](https://karpathy.ai)** – AI researcher known for clear educational content on neural networks and LLMs.
- **[Andrew Ng](https://www.andrewng.org)** – AI pioneer and educator; co-founder of Coursera and DeepLearning.AI.
- **[Chris Olah](https://colah.github.io)** – Interpretability researcher known for beautifully clear explanations of neural networks.
- **[Claude Shannon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Claude_Shannon)** – Father of information theory, the math behind all digital communication.
- **[Demis Hassabis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Demis_Hassabis)** – Co-founder of Google DeepMind; led AlphaGo and AlphaFold, and shared the 2024 Nobel Prize in Chemistry.
- **[Fei-Fei Li](https://profiles.stanford.edu/fei-fei-li)** – Stanford professor who created ImageNet, the dataset that sparked the deep learning boom.
- **[Geoffrey Hinton](https://www.cs.toronto.edu/~hinton/)** – "Godfather of deep learning" and 2024 Nobel Prize winner in Physics.
- **[Jeff Dean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeff_Dean)** – Google engineer behind MapReduce, Bigtable, TensorFlow and much of Google's AI work.
- **[Lilian Weng](https://lilianweng.github.io)** – AI researcher whose blog has some of the best deep dives on LLMs and agents.
- **[Marie Curie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marie_Curie)** – Pioneer of radioactivity research and the first person to win Nobel Prizes in two sciences.
- **[Richard Feynman](https://www.feynmanlectures.caltech.edu)** – Physicist famous for explaining hard ideas simply; the Feynman Lectures are free online.
- **[Richard Hamming](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Hamming)** – Mathematician known for error-correcting codes and the talk "You and Your Research."
- **[Richard Sutton](http://incompleteideas.net)** – Founder of modern reinforcement learning and author of "The Bitter Lesson."
- **[Srinivasa Ramanujan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Srinivasa_Ramanujan)** – Self-taught Indian mathematician whose discoveries still inspire research today.
- **[Terence Tao](https://terrytao.wordpress.com)** – One of the world's leading mathematicians, writing openly about research and math with AI.
- **[Yann LeCun](http://yann.lecun.com)** – Pioneer of convolutional neural networks and Turing Award winner.
- **[Yoshua Bengio](https://yoshuabengio.org)** – Deep learning pioneer and Turing Award winner, focused on AI safety.

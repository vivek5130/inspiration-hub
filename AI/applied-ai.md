## Ads & Marketing

- **[AdCreative.ai](https://www.adcreative.ai)** – Generates ad creatives and predicts which ones are likely to perform best.
- **[Jasper](https://www.jasper.ai)** – AI content platform that helps marketing teams write on-brand copy and campaigns.
- **[Profound](https://www.tryprofound.com)** – Shows brands how they appear in AI answers from ChatGPT, Perplexity and others.
- **[Typeface](https://www.typeface.ai)** – Enterprise marketing platform that creates personalized, on-brand content at scale.

## Coding & App Building

- **[Bolt](https://bolt.new)** – Builds and deploys full-stack web apps from a prompt, right in the browser.
- **[Lovable](https://lovable.dev)** – Turns plain-English descriptions into working web apps, aimed at non-developers.
- **[Replit](https://replit.com)** – Online coding platform whose AI agent builds, runs and deploys apps for you.

## Content & Media

- **[Captions](https://captions.ai)** – AI video editing for creators: captions, cuts, dubbing and talking avatars.
- **[Descript](https://www.descript.com)** – Edit audio and video by editing the transcript, like a text document.
- **[Gamma](https://gamma.app)** – Creates presentations, documents and websites from a short prompt.
- **[HeyGen](https://www.heygen.com)** – AI avatar videos and video translation with lip-synced voices.
- **[Synthesia](https://www.synthesia.io)** – AI avatar videos used by companies for training and internal communication.

## Customer Support

- **[Decagon](https://decagon.ai)** – AI agents that resolve customer support conversations for enterprises.
- **[Fin](https://fin.ai)** – Intercom's AI support agent that answers customer questions across chat, email and phone.
- **[Sierra](https://sierra.ai)** – Branded AI agents that handle customer service for large consumer companies.

## Education & Language

- **[DeepL](https://www.deepl.com)** – AI translation and writing tools known for natural, accurate translations.
- **[Speak](https://www.speak.com)** – Language-learning app where you practice speaking with an AI tutor.

## Finance

- **[Hebbia](https://www.hebbia.com)** – AI that reads and analyzes huge sets of documents for investors and bankers.
- **[Rogo](https://rogo.ai)** – AI analyst for investment banks and financial firms: research, models and decks.

## Healthcare

- **[Abridge](https://www.abridge.com)** – Listens to doctor–patient conversations and writes the clinical notes.
- **[Ambience Healthcare](https://www.ambiencehealthcare.com)** – AI scribe and medical coding assistant for health systems.
- **[Hippocratic AI](https://www.hippocraticai.com)** – Safety-focused AI agents for patient calls like check-ins and follow-ups.
- **[OpenEvidence](https://www.openevidence.com)** – AI medical search engine that answers doctors' questions with cited research; free for clinicians and ad-supported.

## Hiring & Work

- **[Mercor](https://www.mercor.com)** – AI-driven hiring marketplace that matches experts with companies, including AI labs.

## Insights & Research

- **[AlphaSense](https://www.alpha-sense.com)** – Market intelligence platform that searches filings, earnings calls and expert interviews.
- **[Glean](https://www.glean.com)** – AI search and assistant across all of a company's internal tools and documents.
- **[Hex](https://hex.tech)** – Data analytics workspace where AI helps write queries and explore data.

## Legal

- **[Harvey](https://www.harvey.ai)** – AI platform for law firms and legal teams: research, drafting and document review.
- **[Legora](https://legora.com)** – Collaborative AI workspace for lawyers to review, research and draft.

## Sales

- **[11x](https://www.11x.ai)** – AI "digital workers" that handle sales outreach and follow-up.
- **[Clay](https://www.clay.com)** – Combines 150+ data sources with AI research agents to find and enrich sales leads.
- **[Gong](https://www.gong.io)** – Analyzes sales calls and emails to surface insights and forecast deals.

## Voice & Phone

- **[Parloa](https://www.parloa.com)** – AI voice agents for enterprise contact centers.
- **[PolyAI](https://poly.ai)** – Natural-sounding voice assistants that answer phone calls for businesses.
- **[Retell AI](https://www.retellai.com)** – Developer platform for building and deploying AI phone agents.
- **[Vapi](https://vapi.ai)** – Developer API for building custom voice AI agents.

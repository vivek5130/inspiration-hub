- **[Bret Victor](https://worrydream.com)** – Designer and researcher whose talks reimagine how we think and create with computers.
- **[Dan Abramov](https://overreacted.io)** – React core contributor who writes clear, thoughtful posts about JavaScript and UI.
- **[Dan Luu](https://danluu.com)** – Engineer known for deeply researched essays on software, hardware and the tech industry.
- **[David Heinemeier Hansson](https://world.hey.com/dhh)** – Creator of Ruby on Rails and co-founder of 37signals; outspoken on simple software and calm companies.
- **[Dieter Rams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dieter_Rams)** – Industrial designer behind the "ten principles of good design."
- **[Grace Hopper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grace_Hopper)** – Computing pioneer who helped create the first compilers and the COBOL language.
- **[Guillermo Rauch](https://rauchg.com)** – Founder of Vercel and creator of Next.js; writes about developer experience and the web.
- **[John Carmack](https://x.com/ID_AA_Carmack)** – Legendary programmer behind Doom and Quake, now working on AI.
- **[Jony Ive](https://www.lovefrom.com)** – Designer of the iPhone and many Apple products; now runs the design collective LoveFrom.
- **[Julia Evans](https://jvns.ca)** – Writes friendly, approachable explanations and zines about systems and programming.
- **[Kent Beck](https://tidyfirst.substack.com)** – Creator of Extreme Programming and pioneer of test-driven development.
- **[Linus Torvalds](https://github.com/torvalds)** – Creator of Linux and Git.
- **[Martin Fowler](https://martinfowler.com)** – Software author known for writing on refactoring, architecture and agile.
- **[Mitchell Hashimoto](https://mitchellh.com)** – Co-founder of HashiCorp (Terraform, Vagrant) and creator of the Ghostty terminal.
- **[Patrick Collison](https://patrickcollison.com)** – Co-founder of Stripe, known for wide reading and interest in progress.
- **[Paul Graham](https://paulgraham.com)** – Co-founder of Y Combinator whose essays shaped startup thinking.
- **[Sam Altman](https://blog.samaltman.com)** – CEO of OpenAI and former president of Y Combinator.
- **[Simon Willison](https://simonwillison.net)** – Co-creator of Django who writes one of the most useful blogs on using LLMs.

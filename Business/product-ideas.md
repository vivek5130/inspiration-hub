- **[Ask HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/ask)** – Hacker News discussions where people share problems, needs and side projects.
- **[Exploding Topics](https://explodingtopics.com)** – Spots fast-growing trends and topics before they go mainstream.
- **[How to Get Startup Ideas](https://paulgraham.com/startupideas.html)** – Paul Graham's essay on noticing good ideas instead of forcing them.
- **[Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com)** – See new products launching every day and what people are excited about.
- **[YC Requests for Startups](https://www.ycombinator.com/rfs)** – Ideas and problem areas Y Combinator wants founders to work on.

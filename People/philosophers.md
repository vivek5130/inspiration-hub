# Philosophers & Thinkers

Philosophers, writers and thinkers whose ideas shape how we see the world.

- **[Alan Watts](https://alanwatts.org)** – Brought Eastern philosophy to Western audiences through talks and books.
- **[Aristotle](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/aristotle/)** – Greek philosopher whose ideas on logic, ethics and science shaped Western thought.
- **[Buddha](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/buddha/)** – Founder of Buddhism, teaching on suffering, the mind and the middle way.
- **[Chanakya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chanakya)** – Ancient Indian strategist and author of the *Arthashastra* on statecraft and economics.
- **[Charlie Munger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charlie_Munger)** – Warren Buffett's partner, famous for "mental models" and clear thinking.
- **[Confucius](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/confucius/)** – Chinese philosopher whose teachings on ethics and relationships shaped East Asian culture.
- **[Friedrich Nietzsche](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/nietzsche/)** – German philosopher who challenged ideas about morality, meaning and values.
- **[Jiddu Krishnamurti](https://jkrishnamurti.org)** – Indian thinker who spoke about freedom, self-knowledge and understanding the mind.
- **[Laozi](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/laozi/)** – Traditional author of the *Tao Te Ching* and founder of Taoism.
- **[Marcus Aurelius](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/marcus-aurelius/)** – Roman emperor and Stoic whose *Meditations* is still widely read.
- **[Nassim Nicholas Taleb](https://www.fooledbyrandomness.com)** – Writer on risk and uncertainty; author of *The Black Swan* and *Antifragile*.
- **[Naval Ravikant](https://nav.al)** – Investor and thinker known for ideas on wealth, happiness and leverage.
- **[Seneca](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/seneca/)** – Roman Stoic philosopher whose letters give practical advice on living well.
- **[Shane Parrish](https://fs.blog)** – Runs Farnam Street, a site on mental models, decision-making and learning.
- **[Socrates](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/socrates/)** – Greek philosopher known for questioning everything and the Socratic method.
- **[Swami Vivekananda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swami_Vivekananda)** – Indian monk who introduced Vedanta and yoga philosophy to the world.
- **[Yuval Noah Harari](https://www.ynharari.com)** – Historian and author of *Sapiens*, writing about humanity's past and future.

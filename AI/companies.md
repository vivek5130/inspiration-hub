## Major Labs

- **[Alibaba Qwen](https://qwen.ai)** – Alibaba's Qwen family of open-weight language, vision and audio models.
- **[Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com)** – Builds the Claude models, with a strong focus on AI safety research.
- **[DeepSeek](https://www.deepseek.com)** – Chinese lab known for highly efficient open-weight models and detailed technical reports.
- **[Google DeepMind](https://deepmind.google)** – Google's AI research lab, behind AlphaGo, AlphaFold and the Gemini models.
- **[Meta AI](https://ai.meta.com)** – Meta's AI research group, known for the open-weight Llama models and FAIR research.
- **[Mistral AI](https://mistral.ai)** – Paris-based lab building open-weight and commercial models; Europe's leading AI lab.
- **[Moonshot AI](https://www.moonshot.ai)** – Beijing-based lab behind the Kimi models and assistant.
- **[NVIDIA](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/ai/)** – Makes the GPUs and software (like CUDA) that power most AI training and inference.
- **[OpenAI](https://openai.com)** – Creator of ChatGPT and the GPT models, plus image and video generation tools.
- **[xAI](https://x.ai)** – Elon Musk's AI company, building the Grok models.

## Well-Known Startups

- **[Cognition](https://cognition.ai)** – Builds Devin, an autonomous AI software engineer, and the Windsurf code editor.
- **[Cohere](https://cohere.com)** – Enterprise-focused models for search, retrieval and business workflows.
- **[Cursor](https://cursor.com)** – AI-first code editor that understands and edits your whole codebase.
- **[ElevenLabs](https://elevenlabs.io)** – Realistic AI voice generation, text-to-speech and dubbing.
- **[Figure](https://www.figure.ai)** – Builds general-purpose humanoid robots for work and the home.
- **[Isomorphic Labs](https://www.isomorphiclabs.com)** – Google DeepMind spin-out using AI to discover new medicines.
- **[Perplexity](https://www.perplexity.ai)** – AI answer engine that searches the web and cites its sources.
- **[Runway](https://runwayml.com)** – AI video generation and editing tools for creators and filmmakers.
- **[Safe Superintelligence (SSI)](https://ssi.inc)** – Ilya Sutskever's lab focused solely on building safe superintelligence.
- **[Thinking Machines Lab](https://thinkingmachines.ai)** – Research lab founded by former OpenAI CTO Mira Murati, building customizable AI.
- **[World Labs](https://www.worldlabs.ai)** – Fei-Fei Li's company building "spatial intelligence" models that generate 3D worlds.

## Rising & Underrated

- **[Ai2](https://allenai.org)** – Nonprofit research institute releasing fully open models (like OLMo) along with their data and training code.
- **[Baseten](https://www.baseten.co)** – Infrastructure for running AI models in production, fast and at scale.
- **[Black Forest Labs](https://bfl.ai)** – Creators of the FLUX image generation models, founded by original Stable Diffusion researchers.
- **[Cartesia](https://cartesia.ai)** – Real-time voice AI built on state space models instead of Transformers.
- **[Cerebras](https://www.cerebras.ai)** – Makes wafer-scale chips for extremely fast AI training and inference.
- **[EvolutionaryScale](https://evolutionaryscale.ai)** – Protein language models (like ESM3) for understanding and designing new proteins.
- **[Exa](https://exa.ai)** – Search engine built for AI apps and agents, available through an API.
- **[Granola](https://www.granola.ai)** – AI notepad that turns meeting audio and your rough notes into clean summaries.
- **[Krea](https://www.krea.ai)** – Real-time AI tools for generating and editing images and video.
- **[Liquid AI](https://www.liquid.ai)** – MIT spin-out building efficient non-Transformer models that run well on devices.
- **[Modal](https://modal.com)** – Serverless cloud for running AI workloads on GPUs with just Python code.
- **[Nous Research](https://nousresearch.com)** – Open-source research collective behind the Hermes models and decentralized training work.
- **[Physical Intelligence](https://www.physicalintelligence.company)** – General-purpose AI models (the π series) that help robots learn many different tasks.
- **[Prime Intellect](https://www.primeintellect.ai)** – Trains open models using compute pooled from around the world.
- **[Sakana AI](https://sakana.ai)** – Tokyo lab exploring nature-inspired AI, like model merging and "The AI Scientist".
- **[Sarvam AI](https://www.sarvam.ai)** – India-based lab building speech and language models trained for Indian languages.
- **[Skild AI](https://www.skild.ai)** – Building a general-purpose "robot brain" that works across many kinds of robots.

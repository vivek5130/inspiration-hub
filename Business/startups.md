- **[a16z](https://a16z.com)** – Andreessen Horowitz's essays and podcasts on tech, startups and the future.
- **[Acquired](https://www.acquired.fm)** – Podcast telling the full stories of great companies and how they won.
- **[First Round Review](https://review.firstround.com)** – In-depth, practical articles from founders and operators on building companies.
- **[Inc42](https://inc42.com)** – News and analysis on Indian startups and tech.
- **[Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com)** – Community of founders building profitable online businesses, often solo.
- **[Lenny's Newsletter](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com)** – Advice on product, growth and careers from top operators.
- **[Paul Graham's Essays](https://paulgraham.com/articles.html)** – Influential essays on startups, founders and building things people want.
- **[Startup Playbook](https://playbook.samaltman.com)** – Sam Altman's short guide to building a startup.
- **[Startup School](https://www.startupschool.org)** – Free online program from Y Combinator on starting and growing a company.
- **[The Mom Test](https://www.momtestbook.com)** – Short book on how to talk to customers and learn if your idea is any good.
- **[YC Startup Library](https://www.ycombinator.com/library)** – Y Combinator's free library of videos and essays for founders.
- **[YourStory](https://yourstory.com)** – Stories and news about startups and entrepreneurs in India.

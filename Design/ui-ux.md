## Accessibility

- **[The A11Y Project](https://www.a11yproject.com)** – Community-made checklist and guides for making accessible websites.
- **[WCAG](https://www.w3.org/WAI/standards-guidelines/wcag/)** – The official Web Content Accessibility Guidelines from the W3C.

## Color

- **[Color Hunt](https://colorhunt.co)** – Free, curated color palettes picked by designers.
- **[Coolors](https://coolors.co)** – Fast color palette generator and explorer.

## Design Systems & Guidelines

- **[Apple Human Interface Guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines)** – Apple's guidance for designing apps across its platforms.
- **[Material Design](https://m3.material.io)** – Google's open-source design system with components and guidelines.
- **[shadcn/ui](https://ui.shadcn.com)** – Beautiful, copy-paste React components used as the base for many design systems.

## Inspiration Galleries

- **[Behance](https://www.behance.net)** – Adobe's platform where designers share full case studies and projects.
- **[Dribbble](https://dribbble.com)** – Community for sharing UI shots, illustrations and visual design.
- **[Godly](https://godly.website)** – Hand-picked collection of beautiful, modern websites.
- **[Httpster](https://httpster.net)** – Curated, clean and typography-focused website inspiration.
- **[Land-book](https://land-book.com)** – Gallery of landing pages, sorted by style and industry.
- **[Mobbin](https://mobbin.com)** – Large library of real-world mobile and web app screenshots and flows.
- **[Muzli](https://muz.li)** – Daily design inspiration feed, also available as a browser extension.
- **[Page Flows](https://pageflows.com)** – Recordings of full user flows (onboarding, checkout, etc.) from top apps.
- **[Refero](https://refero.design)** – Searchable library of real UI designs and flows from shipped products.
- **[Siteinspire](https://www.siteinspire.com)** – Website showcase with excellent filtering by style and type.

## Tools

- **[Checklist Design](https://www.checklist.design)** – Checklists of what every page, component and flow should include.
- **[Figma](https://www.figma.com)** – The collaborative design tool used by most product teams.

## UX Principles

- **[Laws of UX](https://lawsofux.com)** – Key psychology principles behind good interfaces, explained visually.
- **[Nielsen Norman Group](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/)** – Research-backed articles on usability and user experience.
- **[Refactoring UI](https://www.refactoringui.com)** – Practical design tips for developers, from the creators of Tailwind CSS.

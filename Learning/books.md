## AI & Machine Learning

- **[Deep Learning](https://www.deeplearningbook.org)** – Goodfellow, Bengio and Courville; the classic textbook, free to read online.
- **[Dive into Deep Learning](https://d2l.ai)** – Free interactive book with runnable code for every concept.
- **[Neural Networks and Deep Learning](http://neuralnetworksanddeeplearning.com)** – Michael Nielsen; a free, beautifully clear introduction.

## Business & Ideas

- **[Make Something Wonderful](https://stevejobsarchive.com/book)** – Steve Jobs in his own words; free from the Steve Jobs Archive.
- **[Poor Charlie's Almanack](https://press.stripe.com/poor-charlies-almanack)** – Charlie Munger's talks on clear thinking and mental models.
- **[The Almanack of Naval Ravikant](https://www.navalmanack.com)** – Eric Jorgenson; Naval's ideas on wealth and happiness, free online.
- **[Zero to One](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zero_to_One)** – Peter Thiel; on building companies that create new things.

## Computer Science & Programming

- **[Code: The Hidden Language of Computer Hardware and Software](https://www.codehiddenlanguage.com)** – Charles Petzold; how computers work, from switches up.
- **[Crafting Interpreters](https://craftinginterpreters.com)** – Robert Nystrom; build a programming language from scratch, free online.
- **[Operating Systems: Three Easy Pieces](https://pages.cs.wisc.edu/~remzi/OSTEP/)** – The Arpaci-Dusseaus; a free, readable OS textbook.
- **[Structure and Interpretation of Computer Programs](https://mitpress.mit.edu/9780262510875/structure-and-interpretation-of-computer-programs/)** – Abelson and Sussman; the legendary MIT programming book.
- **[The Pragmatic Programmer](https://pragprog.com/titles/tpp20/the-pragmatic-programmer-20th-anniversary-edition/)** – Thomas and Hunt; timeless habits of good developers.

## Design

- **[The Design of Everyday Things](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Design_of_Everyday_Things)** – Don Norman; why some things are easy to use and others aren't.

## Habits & Thinking

- **[Atomic Habits](https://jamesclear.com/atomic-habits)** – James Clear; how tiny changes build lasting habits.
- **[Deep Work](https://calnewport.com/deep-work-rules-for-focused-success-in-a-distracted-world/)** – Cal Newport; how to focus in a distracted world.
- **[Thinking, Fast and Slow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thinking,_Fast_and_Slow)** – Daniel Kahneman; the two systems that drive how we think and decide.
